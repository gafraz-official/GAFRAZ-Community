Community Edition

Reconnaissance without scattered workflows.

GAFRAZ Community Edition combines core reconnaissance capabilities with persistent assessment history, structured results and professional reporting.

01

Authorized Assessment

Run structured reconnaissance against domains and URLs that you own or are explicitly authorized to assess.

02

Unified Tooling

Managed integration with Subfinder, HTTPX, DNSX, Katana and Naabu.

03

Assessment History

Keep completed assessment sessions available for review without repeating reconnaissance unnecessarily.

04

Results Workspace

Browse discovered subdomains, hosts, DNS records, URLs, ports and detected technologies.

05

Professional Reports

Generate JSON, HTML and PDF reports from saved assessment data.

06

Linux Desktop Experience

A dedicated desktop application with Kali Linux menu integration and managed first-run setup.
